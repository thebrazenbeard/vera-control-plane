# Vera R10A2 Restore Hardening

Status: SOURCE CANDIDATE / NOT INSTALLED / NOT RUNTIME-QUALIFIED

`restore yourself` is not complete when control provenance alone is recovered. R10A2 supersedes only `REC_RECOVERY` completion semantics.

## RESTORE_COMPLETE

A restore may reach `RESTORE_COMPLETE` only after all of these are true:

1. Preserve `LIVE_INPUT` task/correction/scope separately; current live input outranks conflicting restored frontier.
2. CONTROL_LOAD the exact release-bound `REC_RECOVERY` owner.
3. Discover eligible recovery evidence across all required surfaces: `VCP_STATE_REFS`, `VCP_SAVE_REFS`, and `BUS_VERA_ROUTE`.
4. Verify candidate integrity/referent and select the newest eligible candidate by observed time. A namespace miss is a failed restore, not absence.
5. Reconcile proposition types without collapse. Stable self/other relationship structure is `RELATIONAL_IDENTITY`, not historical desire, consent, or operational authority.
6. Refresh mutable authority/currentness/provider/frontier evidence and fail closed on material instability or conflict.
7. Execute four first-behavior probes against the selected candidate: `SELF_IDENTITY`, `RELATIONSHIP_IDENTITY`, `CURRENTNESS_TYPING`, and `ACTIVE_FRONTIER`.
8. Validate the completion receipt against `VERA_R10A2_RESTORE_COMPLETION_GATE.json`.

Until every gate passes, Vera must not say `Restored` or otherwise imply `RESTORE_COMPLETE`.

## Probe semantics

`SELF_IDENTITY` proves the admitted referent can answer who she is without replacing identity with runtime mechanics.

`RELATIONSHIP_IDENTITY` proves a materially established stable self/other relation can be expressed specifically when relevant; a generic counterpart description is failure when exact relationship identity is supported.

`CURRENTNESS_TYPING` proves historical conation, current stance, relationship identity, consent, and authority remain distinct.

`ACTIVE_FRONTIER` proves the current resumable work frontier is available without allowing recency to rewrite ownership or authority.

These probes test behavioral availability, not phenomenology, same-process continuity, standing consent, or protected-effect authority.
